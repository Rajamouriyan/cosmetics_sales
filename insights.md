# 💡 Business Insights Summary

### 1️⃣ Regional Performance
- USA dominates total revenue (~25%).
- Canada and India have consistent mid-tier performance.
- Opportunity: Boost marketing in UK, Australia, and New Zealand.

### 2️⃣ Product Trends
- Tea Tree Moisturizer and Hydrating Face Serums are high-revenue items.
- Serums (like anti-aging, Sunscreen) and Skincare (like vitamin C, Sunscreen) categories drive strong seasonal spikes.

### 3️⃣ Temporal Patterns
- Peak months: March - May.
- Lowest sales: January and February.
- Suggests promotional focus in early Q1 to boost sales during March.
